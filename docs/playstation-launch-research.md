Reviewed 2026-11-17. Market: United States. Rolling launch window: 2025-11-17 through 2026-11-17 inclusive.

The catalog contains 18 product entries and 31 valid configurations: 10 entries launched in the window, plus 8 older hardware families useful for comparison. A collection groups finish variants rather than pretending each finish is a new hardware generation. Product availability and inventory are not established by this research.

## Interpretation and configuration checks

- PS5 is the console family, dated to its original US release on November 12, 2020. The image shows the original chassis. This is explicitly labeled as the family launch; it does not assign an invented day to the November 2023 slim refresh. Disc and Digital Edition are broad edition choices; chassis and SSD capacity must be confirmed on the retailer listing.
- PS5 Pro retains the existing `playstation-5-pro` product slug and `standard` variant. Disc drive and vertical stand are separate accessories.
- Genshin Impact controller uses February 25, 2026 for the US. January 21 was the Asia release and must not be shown as the US date.
- Hyperpop covers fit PS5 slim CFI-2000 and CFI-2100 model groups. They are not PS5 Pro covers. [Official US fit details](https://direct.playstation.com/en-us/buy-accessories/ps5-console-covers-model-group-slim-techno-red).
- Wolverine covers permit exactly three model/finish combinations: PS5 slim Battle Yellow, PS5 Pro Battle Yellow, and PS5 Pro Adamantium. No slim Adamantium configuration is offered.
- Limited-edition DualSense releases change the design, not the underlying feature generation. Haptics/adaptive triggers depend on game and device support. [Common controller feature documentation](https://www.playstation.com/en-us/accessories/dualsense-wireless-controller/).
- Portal, DualSense Edge, PULSE Elite and PULSE Explore have White and Midnight Black variants. Their family dates are earlier than the black finish release. [Midnight Black collection launched February 20, 2025](https://blog.playstation.com/2025/01/07/new-midnight-black-collection-of-ps5-accessories-launches-next-month/), outside the window.
- Portal cloud streaming reflects current official product documentation rather than the original 2023 restriction. Eligible PlayStation Plus Premium membership, account, region and connection are required. Remote Play separately requires a PS5 and compatible games.
- Main images are representative photographs with explicit finish descriptions. Hyperpop and Wolverine controller variants additionally provide their actual variant photograph. The White core-accessory photographs should not be represented as photographs of the Midnight Black selection.

## Excluded or deliberately bounded coverage

- No video games, merchandise, third-party licensed accessories, refreshed retailer bundles, regional-only hardware or software feature updates are counted as hardware launches. Bundled game codes are described only as contents of an official hardware bundle.
- The generic standalone DualSense is not given a fabricated first-sale date. Official sources establish its 2020 generation but this pass did not confirm its earliest US standalone sale date. The catalog still includes DualSense Edge and six special-edition DualSense entries.
- The monitor is included at its August 27, 2026 US launch. The shared June 1 hardware article was updated to delay FlexStrike, so the old announced August 6 date is not treated as an actual release. PULSE Elevate is likewise not asserted to have shipped without confirmed final availability.
- [GTA VI controllers](https://blog.playstation.com/2026/09/03/first-look-at-the-grand-theft-auto-vi-limited-edition-dualsense-wireless-controllers/) and the new [PULSE headset](https://blog.playstation.com/2026/09/15/introducing-the-next-line-of-ps5-audio-products-playstation-pulse-and-pulse-edge-wireless-headsets/) are scheduled for November 19, 2026, after the snapshot date; PULSE Edge is later still.
- [Gran Turismo controller](https://blog.playstation.com/2026/10/14/gran-turismo-7s-spec-iv-part-1-launches-today-and-a-first-look-at-dualsense-wireless-controller-gran-turismo-limited-edition/) is scheduled for December 10, 2026. [Prism Collection](https://blog.playstation.com/2026/10/28/first-look-inside-the-prism-collection-with-three-new-transparent-designs-for-ps5-accessories/) is scheduled for February 4, 2027. Preorders do not make either a completed launch.
- Recent release coverage was checked against the official [peripherals and accessories archive](https://blog.playstation.com/category/peripherals-accessories/) and [DualSense archive](https://blog.playstation.com/tag/dualsense/). This is a curated US hardware catalog, not an assertion that every historic edition, retailer SKU or global release is included.
