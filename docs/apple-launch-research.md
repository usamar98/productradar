# Apple launch catalog research

Verified on 2026-11-17 for the inclusive US launch window **2025-11-17 through 2026-11-17**. This catalog contains 24 product generations and 251 curated configurations. Product identity, launch evidence, features, and option constraints use official Apple sources. No prices, inventory claims, or retailer SKU mappings are inferred.

## Coverage and date semantics

- Includes iPhone, iPad, Mac notebooks/desktops/displays, Apple Watch, AirPods, Home, and AirTag hardware evidenced in the window.
- iPhone 18 Pro and Pro Max are separate products. Size options group iPad Air and MacBook Air. March Liquid Retina XDR and November PROLED XDR MacBook Pro launches are separate generations.
- AirPods Max 2 has a date precision exception: its official announcement says orders open **March 25, 2026**, with retail availability **early April**. The JSON uses the verified order date with an explicit launchLabel, not an invented retail date. Apple’s education price list labels March 31 a Pricing Date, which is not proof of first retail availability.
- AirTag is officially described as available online on **January 26, 2026**, with Apple Store locations later that week. The launchLabel preserves that distinction.
- Other launchDate values are the announced first customer/store availability dates, not announcement or preorder dates.
- No new 2026 Vision Pro, base iPad, Watch SE, AirPods Pro, or full-size HomePod generation was verified. The M5 iPad Pro/Vision Pro and September 2025 iPhones/Watch/AirPods Pro fall before this window and are deliberately excluded.
- Apple Home Collection accessories advertised for December 2026 or early 2027 are outside the window. Cases, bands, cables, adapters, engraving, third-party accessories, enterprise multipacks, and seasonal accessory recolors are not a comprehensive SKU inventory here.

## Configuration evidence and constraints

- These are **curated valid configurations, not every configure-to-order combination**. A variant is an explicit option tuple; consumers must filter against tuples rather than freely cross-multiply independently available option values. Variant slugs are catalog identifiers, not manufacturer part numbers.
- iPhone Duo retains its existing product slug and all eight storage/finish slugs. The announcement verifies 256GB, 512GB, 1TB, and 2TB in Night Sky and Star White: [Duo announcement](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2026/09/apple-unveils-iphone-duo/).
- iPhone 17e has three finishes and two capacities. iPhone 18 Pro/Pro Max each have four finishes and four capacities per their linked announcements.
- iPad Air combines both sizes, four finishes, four capacities, and Wi-Fi or Wi-Fi + Cellular. The launch release supports this complete option matrix.
- iPad mini restricts nano-texture glass to 256GB and 512GB. Its chip differs by storage: the smaller capacities do not have the same CPU/Neural Engine configuration as 512GB. [iPad mini specs](https://www.apple.com/ipad-mini/specs/) and [Apple Support specs](https://support.apple.com/en-us/149482). Some indexed Apple store results still described the previous A17 Pro generation; those stale results were not used.
- MacBook Neo: 8GB memory with 256GB or 512GB in four finishes; Touch ID belongs to the 512GB model. [Neo specs](https://www.apple.com/macbook-neo/specs/).
- MacBook Air: each size/finish offers the representative 16GB/512GB and 24GB/1TB tuples. The 13-inch base has an eight-core GPU while 15-inch and the higher configuration have a ten-core GPU. The feature text does not imply one GPU configuration across all variants. [Air specs](https://www.apple.com/macbook-air/specs/).
- M5 Pro/Max MacBook Pro: standard-glass base tuples use M5 Pro with 24GB/1TB and M5 Max with 36GB/2TB in both sizes and finishes. M5 Pro CPU/GPU counts differ by size; labels name the chip family. M6 configurations use 16/24/32GB and 1/2TB with standard glass. [Liquid Retina XDR specs](https://www.apple.com/macbook-pro/liquid-retina-xdr-display/specs/) and [PROLED XDR purchase options](https://www.apple.com/shop/buy-mac/macbook-pro-proled-xdr).
- iMac: 16GB/256GB, standard glass and desktop stand, both CPU/GPU configurations and seven finishes. [iMac specs](https://www.apple.com/imac/specs/) and [Apple store](https://www.apple.com/shop/buy-mac/imac).
- Mac mini: representative M6 16GB/256GB, 16GB/512GB, 24GB/512GB and M5 Pro 24GB/512GB. [Mac mini store](https://www.apple.com/shop/buy-mac/mac-mini/).
- Mac Studio: M5 Max 18-core CPU/32-core GPU/36GB/512GB and M5 Ultra 30-core CPU/64-core GPU/96GB/1TB standard tuples. [Studio specs](https://www.apple.com/mac-studio/specs/).
- [Apple US education price list](https://www.apple.com/education/pricelists/pdfs/Apple_US_Education_Institution_Price_List.pdf) and [NASPO Apple price list](https://www.apple.com/education/purchase/contracts/docs/NASPO_PSS_Apple_Branded.pdf) corroborate Mac configurations, not retail launch dates. They are rolling documents and may change.
- Studio Display: two glass treatments with tilt, tilt/height, or VESA. Studio Display XDR: two glass treatments with tilt/height or VESA. Neither catalog entry implies a Mac is included.
- Watch Series 12 intentionally exposes aluminum cases only in the four verified finishes, both sizes and both connectivity options. Other case materials exist but are outside the curated matrix. Ultra 4 exposes the two titanium finishes. Watch variants identify **case configurations**, not complete band SKUs; compatible band and fit are chosen at the retailer, as the descriptions say.
- AirPods 5: USB-C Charging Case or Wireless Charging Case. On-stem volume control is attributed only to the latter. AirPods Max 2 uses the five verified finishes.
- HomeView: tabletop Silver/Starlight/Rose Pink/Space Gray; wall-mounted Silver/Space Gray only. Wall-mount hardware is separate. [HomeView announcement](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2026/10/meet-apple-homeview-a-new-way-to-enjoy-entertainment-and-manage-the-home/).
- HomePod mini 2: five finishes. Siri AI was described as a future software update at launch, so it is not represented as a shipping feature. Surround playback requires the new Apple TV 4K and compatible paired speakers.
- Apple TV 4K fourth generation: 128GB Wi-Fi + Ethernet. [Apple Support specifications](https://support.apple.com/en-us/149359). AirTag: one-pack and four-pack.

## Images

Official product or Newsroom Open Graph photos were downloaded unmodified to the owned apple-* asset paths. Each image.sourceUrl stores its precise origin. They are representative product photography, not a selected-variant preview. iPhone 18 Pro and Pro Max share Apple’s two-model lineup image; alt text states both models. AirTag photography shows a separately sold key ring, identified in alt text. iPhone Duo reuses the existing verified hero asset.

All newly downloaded images are below 400KB. Image identity and selected key assets were visually checked. Public manufacturer assets remain subject to Apple’s applicable usage terms; this catalog does not assert ownership or endorsement.
